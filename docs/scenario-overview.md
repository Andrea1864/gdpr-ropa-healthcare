# MedCore Health — Company Overview

## Company Profile
- **Name**: MedCore Health S.L.
- **Sector**: Private Healthcare
- **Employees**: 30
- **Location**: Barcelona, Spain
- **Services**: General medicine, specialist consultations, 
  diagnostic testing, physiotherapy
- **Role as Data Controller**: Yes
- **DPO appointed**: Yes (mandatory — large scale processing 
  of special category health data under Art. 9 GDPR)

## Data Processing Context
MedCore Health processes special category data (health data) 
as its core business activity. This triggers additional 
obligations under:

- GDPR (EU) 2016/679 — Art. 9 special category data
- LOPDGDD (Spain) — Organic Law 3/2018
- Ley 41/2002 — Patient Autonomy Law (clinical records)
- Real Decreto 2210/1995 — Notifiable diseases
- Ley 31/1995 — Occupational Health & Safety

| Department | Processing Activities |
