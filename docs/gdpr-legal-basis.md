# GDPR Legal Bases Applied — MedCore Health

## Art. 6 GDPR — Lawfulness of Processing
| Code | Legal Basis | When Used at MedCore |
|---|---|---|
| Art. 6(1)(a) | Consent | Marketing, optional surveys |
| Art. 6(1)(b) | Contract | Patient registration, appointments |
| Art. 6(1)(c) | Legal obligation | Clinical record retention, notifiable diseases, occupational health |
| Art. 6(1)(f) | Legitimate interest | Fraud prevention, IT security, appointment optimisation |

## Art. 9 GDPR — Special Category Data
Health data requires an additional legal basis under Art. 9:

| Code | Legal Basis | When Used at MedCore |
|---|---|---|
| Art. 9(2)(a) | Explicit consent | Optional genetic testing, research |
| Art. 9(2)(h) | Healthcare provision | Clinical records, diagnosis, treatment |
| Art. 9(2)(i) | Public health | Notifiable disease reporting |
| Art. 9(2)(b) | Employment & social security | Employee occupational health |

## Key Principle — Dual Legal Basis
Every processing activity involving health data at MedCore 
requires TWO legal bases:
1. One from Art. 6 (general lawfulness)
2. One from Art. 9 (special category legitimacy)
